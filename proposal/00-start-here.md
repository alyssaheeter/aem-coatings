# Start Here

Welcome, Andres — and thanks for the chance to work on this.

This space holds everything for AEM's website and marketing proposal in one place. It's
built so you can read top to bottom, or jump to the part you care about. Take your time.

## How to navigate

The proposal lives in a single document with a handful of pages (you'll see them in the
sidebar on the left):

1. **Start Here** — you're reading it.
2. **The Proposal** — the short version: where AEM is now, and where this takes you.
3. **Scope of Work** — exactly what gets built, and what I'll need from you.
4. **Service Packages** — four ways to work together, side by side.
5. **Pricing Schedule** — the numbers, and what's a one-time cost vs. monthly.
6. **Design Concepts** — three live homepage directions you can click through right now.
7. **Why Alyssa** — a little about me and past work.

When you're ready to move forward, there's one task called **"Approve & Select"** — that's
where you pick your package and design direction. That's the only place you need to *do*
anything; everything else is just for reading.

## What I'll need to begin

Nothing yet — read first. When you're ready, the kickoff list (on the Approve & Select
task) covers it, but the short version is: a few real photos of your work, your
coating/warranty details, and access to your Google Business Profile and domain/host.
