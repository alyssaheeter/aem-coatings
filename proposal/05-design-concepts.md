# Design Concepts

Here are **three live homepage directions** for AEM — same brand, same real services and
contact details, three different feels. They're clickable, working pages (not pictures), so
go ahead and scroll, tap the buttons, and see them on your phone.

> **How to pick:** Don't overthink the copy or photos — those are placeholders we'll replace
> with your real words and pictures. Focus on the *feel*: which one looks and feels most
> like AEM to you? That's the one we build out.

### Concept A — Heritage Trust
Badge-forward, warm and editorial. Leads with credibility and the family-run story — built
to feel established and trustworthy.
**Live preview:** https://alyssaheeter.github.io/aem-coatings/concepts/a.html

### Concept B — Bold & Local
High-contrast and conversion-punchy. Big "219" local pride, prominent before/after, and a
call-to-action everywhere a customer might be ready to act.
**Live preview:** https://alyssaheeter.github.io/aem-coatings/concepts/b.html

### Concept C — Clean Service-First
Minimal and fast, with a side-by-side service split and an instant-quote form right at the
top. Built to convert with zero clutter.
**Live preview:** https://alyssaheeter.github.io/aem-coatings/concepts/c.html

---

Whatever you pick is the *starting direction* — the finished design (your real photos,
final colors, exact wording) is done in the build phase after you choose.

**You choose your direction (A / B / C) on the "Approve & Select" task.**
