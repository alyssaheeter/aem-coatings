# Scope of Work

This is what's included, phase by phase. What's in *your* engagement depends on the package
you choose (see **Service Packages**) — this page is the full menu so nothing is a surprise.

## Phase 1 — Foundation (the website) · included in every package

**Deliverables:**
- A custom, mobile-responsive website (lean-complete — the pages customers actually need):
  - Home, Services (coatings + carpet care), About, Reviews/Proof, Contact/Quote.
- Real business details wired throughout: `219-255-0949`, your real email, service area.
- **Click-to-call** and a **quote-request form** that reaches you.
- Before/after photo section and a reviews area ready for your content.
- Both service lines presented so they cross-sell each other.
- Basic on-page SEO (titles, descriptions, local keywords) and fast, clean markup.
- Connect the site to your domain and hosting, and launch.

## Phase 2 — Reputation *(Reputation package and up)*
- Google Business Profile cleanup and upkeep.
- A simple system to ask happy customers for reviews and respond to the ones you get.

## Phase 3 — Content & Growth *(Growth package and up)*
- Blog posts (2 / month) on local, useful topics that help you show up in search.
- Light social posting to keep AEM visible.

## Phase 4 — Full Marketing Partner *(top package)*
- Full social media management.
- Monthly performance report in plain English.
- Ongoing conversion tweaks to the site based on what's working.

---

## Out of scope (unless we agree separately)
- Paid ads / ad spend (Google or Meta) and the budget behind them.
- Logo redesign or full rebrand (we'll use your existing brand assets).
- Professional photography or videography on-site.
- E-commerce / online payment checkout.
- Writing or verifying **warranty terms** or any product/coating claims — those come from
  you (see below).
- Third-party software fees beyond what's noted in the Pricing Schedule.

## What I'll need from you (client responsibilities)
- **Photos** — real before/after shots of your work (the more, the better).
- **Coating & warranty details** — the exact products you use and the warranty/guarantee
  language you want on the site, in your words. I won't invent these.
- **Approvals** — timely yes/no on drafts so we keep moving.
- **Access** — your Google Business Profile, and your domain/host login (or your registrar
  details so we can point the domain).
- **The "other services" question** — anything beyond coatings and carpet care you want
  represented. `[CAPTURE: other services]`
