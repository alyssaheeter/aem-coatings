# The Proposal

## The short version

AEM does good work — tub and tile refinishing and carpet care — for families across
Northwest Indiana. The problem is that almost nobody can tell that from your website right
now. This proposal fixes that: a lean, complete website that looks like the real business
behind it, plus optional marketing that keeps new customers coming in after launch.

## Situation & problem — a quick audit of the current site

Your current site (aemsurfacecare.com) is an unconfigured WordPress + Flatsome demo. It
isn't broken so much as *unfinished* — and unfortunately it shows the kind of details that
make a first-time visitor hesitate:

- **Wrong contact info.** A leftover Norway phone number (`+47 900 99 000`) and a
  placeholder `youremail@gmail.com` — not your real `219-255-0949` or your real email.
- **Placeholder everywhere.** "Lorem ipsum" body text where your story and services
  should be.
- **A "Flatsome Theme" footer** and a **demo shop/cart navigation** that doesn't match a
  refinishing-and-carpet business.
- **No real proof.** No before/after photos, no reviews, nothing that shows the quality of
  the work.

None of that reflects AEM. The good news: it's entirely fixable, and you don't need a
giant, complicated website to fix it.

## Proposed approach

Build a **lean-complete** site — every page a customer actually needs, nothing they don't:

- A clear homepage that says who you are, what you do, and how to reach you.
- Both service lines represented — **coatings** *and* **carpet care** — so each one
  cross-sells the other.
- Real contact details, click-to-call, and an easy way to request a quote.
- Room for the proof that wins jobs: before/after photos and reviews.

The whole thing is built around your honest, family-run, no-corners-cut voice — credible
without being stiff.

## Objectives

1. **Replace the demo** with a site that's accurate, professional, and unmistakably AEM.
2. **Make it easy to contact you** — calls and quote requests, from any device.
3. **Show the work** with before/after proof and customer reviews.
4. **Cross-sell both lines** so a carpet customer learns about refinishing, and vice versa.
5. **Set up for repeat business** — reviews, Google presence, and an optional
   "Fresh Start Club" so customers come back.

> Positioning, in one line: the **approachable, honest craftsman** — where the offer is
> built on **warranty and scope, not the lowest price**.
